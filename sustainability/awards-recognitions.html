Sarawak Chief Minister's Environmental Award

CMEA Heritage & History

Prior to its rebranding as the PSEA, the Sarawak Chief Minister's Environmental Award (CMEA) recognized SALCRA's estates for high standards in environmental stewardship, resource management, and social responsibility.

Award Editions Timeline

10th Premier's Sarawak Environmental Award (PSEA)

2021/2022

Marked the official rebranding to PSEA. SALCRA showcased advanced sustainability policies and secured 12 individual achievements/categories.

9th Sarawak Chief Minister's Environmental Award (CMEA)

2019/2020

Recognized SALCRA's estates for maintaining excellent ecological practices during the challenging biennial review.

7th Sarawak Chief Minister's Environmental Award (CMEA)

2015/2016

Reinforced the adoption of environmental practices across our palm oil mill and nursery divisions.

6th Sarawak Chief Minister's Environmental Award (CMEA)

2013/2014

Initial benchmark recognition establishing SALCRA as a pioneer in sustainable consolidation of Native Customary Rights (NCR) land.
