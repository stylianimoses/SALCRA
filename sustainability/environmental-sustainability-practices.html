MSPO Certified Since 2018

Malaysian Sustainable Palm Oil (MSPO)

The Malaysian Sustainable Palm Oil (MSPO) is a Malaysian certification scheme standard to address the sustainability aspects of the local palm oil industry, as well as an ethical platform to boost world market access.

Sustainability Assured

Compliance with stringent national standards ensures that all consolidated land, farming, and processing practices maintain ecological balance and benefit landowners and local communities alike.

Nursery Compliance

Code of Good Nursery Practices (CoPN)

Responsible production starts at the root. Standard operating procedures are rigidly enforced in our nurseries to raise robust, disease-free oil palm seedlings.
