Supplier Guide

Contractor License & UPKJ Guides

Review the license categorizations required to bid for SALCRA services and engineering works.

**Head IV (Agriculture, Forestry & Husbandry)** is the official classification under Sarawak's UPKJ (Unit Pendaftaran Kontraktor dan Juruperunding) licensing board covering agricultural services.

To supply tools, agricultural equipment, fertilizers, or services like AET project implements, suppliers must hold a valid UPKJ registration certificate matching this classification.

Fees are paid online via E-Perolehan and are **non-refundable**. Suppliers must present the official printed e-receipt and a copy of their UPKJ license certificate when collecting physical document blueprints or tender specs at the counter.

Tenders/Quotations close exactly at **12:00 Noon** on the designated closing date. Late submissions, regardless of courier delays or postal lag, **will not be accepted** and will be returned unopened.

Submission Requirements Checklist

Suppliers submitting physical documents to the Tender Secretariat must enclose their bid package inside a sealed envelope containing the following documents:

  • 1 UPKJ License: Copy of a valid UPKJ certificate (verified matching category B/C, Head IV).
  • 2 Receipt Copy: Copy of E-Perolehan purchase transaction printout.
  • 3 Company Profile: Certified audited financials and project records (for large tenders).
  • 4 Sealed Envelope: Submit in a wax-sealed envelope with the Reference Number and Title written on the top-left corner.
